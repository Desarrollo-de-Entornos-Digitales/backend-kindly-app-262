- (decision) Brief approved by user. Responsive mobile-first web app chosen as baseline (pending tutor confirmation). Added recommendations for database persistence, volunteer hours tracking, cause categories, and attendance confirmation.
- (decision) Updated requirements: 1. Attendance tracking simplified to binary check-in/cause completion (removing exact hours calculation). 2. Added 3rd role: Administrator (super-user managing users, enabling/disabling accounts, approving organization verification, and role/permission assignments).
- (decision) Parked future enhancement ideas (Certificate PDF generation, CSV attendance export, emergency contacts, admin KPI dashboard) in Addendum for future review during PRD/Architecture phases.
- (decision) Decision: Rejected open marketplace/classifieds post board to avoid scope creep and dilution. Instead, integrated structured in-cause resource/item needs (Organizations specify required items; Volunteers can indicate items they can contribute during application).
